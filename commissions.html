PORTRAIT COMMISSIONS

I accept a limited number of portrait commissions throughout the year. Each portrait is created from photographic reference, with an emphasis on likenes, character and atmosphere.

To inquire about a portrait commission please send a request through the Commission Form.


What I Offer

Graphite or Charcoal Drawing - from €160

  • -Original drawings on paper, focusing on expression, structure, and light.

Oil Painting - from €320

  • -Original oil paintings on fine linen canvas, made with archival artist-grade materials.


The Process

  • -Send me a few photographs of the person you would like portrayed, along with a brief description of what you have in mind.
  • -I will help you choose the appropriate size and medium based on your reference and preferences.
  • -A 30% deposit secures your commission. The remaining balance is due before delivery.
  • -Portraits are generally available as single-subject, head-and-shoulders compositions. Larger formats, multiple subjects, or more complex compositions can be discussed separately.
